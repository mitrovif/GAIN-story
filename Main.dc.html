413 examples were reported to the GAIN Survey between 2021 and 2025. Each circle is one example.

Adoption of the Recommendations remained high in 2025, with 81 per cent of country-led and 74 per cent of institution-led examples applying them, despite a challenging landscape and a decline in country-led examples.

STATELESS PERSONS · IROSS · 2023–2025

14 examples applied IROSS alone, all reported after IROSS was adopted in 2023.

LATEST ROUNDOVERALL TRENDS · 2025 · IRRS, IRIS AND IROSS

IRIS-only examples surpassed IRRS-only examples for the first time since 2022, with 34 and 27 examples respectively, driven by a rise in institution-led IRIS implementation.

LATEST ROUNDEach chart first shows the newest results, GAIN 2025, then opens up to ALL ROUNDSsince 2021. Examples from other rounds, or outside a chart, stay in the background as faint circles. The 91 examples using the Recommendations in 2025 form the ring: IRIS 34, IRRS 27, mixed use 24, undetermined 4 and IROSS 2.

Each mixed-use circle is split into the Recommendations it combines. All three Recommendations combined was the most common mix (11), followed by IRRS and IRIS (10) and IRRS and IROSS (3). No IRIS and IROSS examples were reported in 2025.

The inner ring splits them by example lead: 51 country-led and 40 institution-led.
